\section{Analysis}
\label{sec:analysis}

We organize transfer analysis around three questions: which external task
families benefit from \trace training, whether breadth across program
contracts is associated with transfer beyond synthetic renderings, and where
procedural visual style limits generalization. Negative transfer is reported
alongside gains rather than being absorbed into an aggregate score.

\subsection{Model-conditional task difficulty}

\trace does not assign one intrinsic difficulty score across its heterogeneous
task contracts.  Under a fixed model checkpoint and evaluation protocol,
however, empirical solve rate provides a practical difficulty proxy: it can
identify tasks near the current policy's competence frontier, exclude tasks
that are uniformly solved or failed, and initialize a curriculum.  This
ordering is deliberately model conditional.  It should be recomputed as the
policy changes rather than interpreted as a permanent property of the task.
