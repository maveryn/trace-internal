\trace can support open research on multimodal reasoning by making task
definitions, labels, and rewards inspectable and reproducible. Procedural data
may reduce dependence on sensitive or copyrighted source imagery for some
experiments and can expose model weaknesses through controlled variation.
However, synthetic environments can encode the designers' assumptions, create
misleading confidence about real-world robustness, and consume substantial
compute when used for large-scale RL. Visual reasoning capabilities can also be
applied to surveillance or automated decision systems. Responsible use
therefore requires clear intended-use, coverage, licensing, maintenance, and
failure-mode documentation. Performance in \trace should not be interpreted as
evidence of safety or general real-world competence.
