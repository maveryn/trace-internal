\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\trace treats multidomain visual reasoning data as a formal environment of scene
grammars, executable task contracts, and replayable verifier traces. This
design connects taxonomy, generation, validation, and answer-based RLVR in one
environment. By keeping visual grammar separate from reasoning objectives,
\trace supports controlled extension and regeneration without obscuring the
unit being sampled, trained, or evaluated.
