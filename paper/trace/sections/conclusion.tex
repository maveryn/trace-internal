\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\trace treats multidomain visual reasoning data as a formal environment of scene
grammars, executable task contracts, and replayable verifier traces. This
design connects taxonomy, generation, validation, and answer-based RLVR in one
environment. By keeping visual grammar separate from reasoning objectives,
\trace supports controlled extension and regeneration without obscuring the
unit being sampled, trained, or evaluated. In answer-only RLVR experiments,
64,000 generated instances improve 24-benchmark macro-averages by 3.51 points
at 3B and 4.06 points at 7B, with positive 7B transfer on every benchmark in
the suite. These results support procedural task breadth as a practical source
of transferable visual-reasoning experience while leaving mixture attribution,
difficulty-aware curricula, and effective use of audit annotations open.
