\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The taxonomy is designed and curated by humans, so its task boundaries remain
judgment calls even when guided by program contracts. Procedural renderers can
introduce style regularities unlike natural images, and broad task count does
not guarantee complete coverage of real visual reasoning. Metadata verifiers
guarantee consistency with the generated world, not that every rendering is
equally easy to perceive. \trace also does not define task-local
difficulty ladders or a universal ordering across tasks.  Solve rate depends on
the evaluated model and protocol, while structural factors such as distractor
quality in multiple choice and derivation depth in geometry are not directly
commensurate.  Future work could combine contract-preserving complexity axes
within tasks with cross-task prerequisite relations inferred from executable
programs and empirical learning curves. \trace additionally contains
projected visual annotations used during task auditing, but establishing
whether and how they should be used as training targets remains future work.
