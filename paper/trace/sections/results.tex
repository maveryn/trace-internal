\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

The primary comparison pairs each base Qwen2.5-VL checkpoint with its
\trace-trained counterpart under the same benchmark decoding and scoring
protocol. Benchmark-level macro-averages summarize transfer without allowing
larger benchmark datasets to dominate the aggregate, while individual
benchmark scores retain regressions and task-family differences.

Comparisons with independently trained visual RLVR checkpoints use the same
evaluation protocol but are treated as contextual references rather than
compute-matched controls because their training data and optimization budgets
differ.
